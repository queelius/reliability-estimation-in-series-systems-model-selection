## Key Findings

1. **Bias-detectability alignment**: prediction bias grows sub-linearly, LRT power super-linearly — the test lacks power precisely where misspecification is harmless
2. **MTTF bias < 1%** through actual CV ≈ 14%; 1% threshold not crossed until CV ≈ 20%
3. **Full model has lower MSE** than reduced even at CV = 0 (nonlinear MTTF functional doesn't propagate variance reduction)
4. **Adaptive RMSE within 2.5%** of always-full at n ≥ 500 (8-18% at n = 100)
5. **LRT well-calibrated** (4.6-6.8% Type I error) where AIC is liberal (~2x) and BIC over-conservative (~0%)
